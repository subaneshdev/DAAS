# DaaS.media design direction

Original editorial agency design in black, pink and warm white. Large Manrope headings, DM Sans body copy, graphic star and building artwork, clear service navigation and visible enquiry calls to action. Responsive layouts use semantic sections and native disclosure controls. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences.

The website connects digital campaigns with creators, communities and physical distribution. Real estate is the first specialty. No fabricated results or client logos. See docs/SEO-PLAN.md for sitemap and messaging.
