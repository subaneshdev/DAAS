# DAAS website direction

## Objective
Turn visitors into qualified project conversations by explaining one clear proposition: DAAS helps brands reach people through coordinated digital and physical distribution.

## Visual language
Black provides contrast, warm white gives the content breathing room, and pink marks moments of emphasis. Large headlines, fine dividing lines, an animated distribution ticker, original campaign mockups and compact floating navigation give the site an editorial agency feel.

The reference informs typographic scale, spacing and navigation. All DAAS copy and artwork is original.

## Visitor journey
1. Hero: “Get seen. Everywhere.” immediately explains the ambition.
2. About: introduce DAAS as a distribution partner.
3. Services: distinguish digital, physical and connected campaigns.
4. Possibilities: show two illustrative campaign directions with expandable detail.
5. Connected-world statement: reinforce the digital/physical proposition.
6. Approach: Discover, Design, Distribute, Develop.
7. Questions: explain scope, fulfilment boundaries, budgets and timelines.
8. Enquiry: collect a brief and hand it to the visitor's email application.

## Content principles
Use direct language and practical service descriptions. Avoid unverified performance statistics, invented clients and unconfirmed delivery coverage. Start with campaign concepts until approved client work can replace them.

## First release
Responsive static website, original brand styling, service disclosures, campaign dialogs, project enquiry dialog, email draft generation, brief download, privacy information, search metadata and self-hosted fonts.

## Future extensions
Direct enquiry delivery and CRM integration; verified case studies; region-specific service pages; analytics after privacy choices are agreed; a publishing system if frequent content updates become necessary.
