Each service has a separate problem, scope, process, illustrative use case, FAQs and related links. Search phrases are used naturally. Individual service content and exact metadata are editable in `src/content`. No ranking, sales or audience guarantees, invented offices, testimonials or client results.

## Technical foundation

Server-rendered content; absolute page titles; unique descriptions; canonical URLs with trailing slashes; Open Graph and Twitter metadata; generated social image; sitemap and robots; Organization, WebSite, BreadcrumbList and Service structured data where appropriate. Organization details contain only brand name, site, logo and confirmed email. No unverified postal address, reviews or phone number.

Local fonts, responsive layouts, semantic landmarks, skip link, native disclosure FAQs, reduced-motion support and accessible form validation. No analytics installed; establish a consent and measurement plan before adding third-party tags.

## Reference scope

Design and service breadth were informed by https://dontmatter.eu/services/digital-marketing. Copy and implementation are original. ChatGPT Ads access and campaign suitability must be checked against https://developers.openai.com/ads before proposing a launch.
