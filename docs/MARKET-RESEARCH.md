## Geography and duplication flags

- Noida Extension is commonly known as Greater Noida West. Retain the user's original research label, but do not publish duplicate pages under both Noida and Greater Noida. Verify project jurisdiction and use one canonical destination. See the [NMRC project structure](https://nmrcnoida.com/Projects/Upcoming-projects) and the [reported authority renaming](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/noida/noida-extension-to-now-be-called-greater-noida-west/articleshow/16403369.cms).
- Research Mohali and Zirakpur separately from Chandigarh. They are separate planning markets; do not present them as Chandigarh sectors. Consult [Punjab planning material](https://puda.punjab.gov.in/sites/default/files/Zirakpur_rpt_2011.pdf).
- Technopark is an institutional/campus search topic connected to Kazhakkoottam. Avoid two interchangeable locality pages. Check the [official campus address](https://technopark.in/contact-technopark).
- Panvel and New Panvel are not automatically interchangeable boundaries. Confirm the actual project site before selecting a campaign catchment.
- OMR, Sarjapur Road, Sohna Road, Dwarka Expressway, Yamuna Expressway, SG Highway, Ajmer Road, Shaheed Path, Wardha Road and Super Corridor are corridor-style research labels. Define project-specific catchments rather than treating each as a precise municipal area.
- New Town and Rajarhat need careful boundary and audience distinction. Rushikonda and Madhurawada can overlap in broad property descriptions. MIHAN requires relevant authority/project checks. Old or draft master plans must not be presented as evidence of completed projects.

## Work needed before publishing a local page

For each market, verify locality names, jurisdiction and catchment; record dated official sources; research language and buyer questions; identify actual media and creator availability through agreed outreach; confirm shoot/activation feasibility; define relevant campaign formats and enquiry routing. Do not state costs, inventory, commute times, future infrastructure or buyer demand without current evidence.

A city page needs distinct buyer questions, an actionable channel plan, verified delivery scope, original relevant content and a useful reason to exist apart from its city name. Locality pages need even more specific material. An approved, anonymised campaign example can help once one exists.

Potential future structure: `/real-estate-marketing/{city}/` and only where justified `/real-estate-marketing/{city}/{locality}/`. The current real estate page targets Chennai; do not create a competing Chennai page without first revising the parent page's national intent. Consolidate aliases with redirects rather than duplicate pages. Index only complete, substantive pages.
