# DaaS organic growth: execution playbook

Prepared 28 September 2026. This is a working plan, not a ranking or lead forecast. No advertising spend or paid backlink budget is assumed. Production and delivery still take time and may have costs.

## Positioning and order of work

Keep the homepage focused on digital marketing agency in Chennai. Real estate remains the first specialty. Restaurant demonstration work is the next concrete sample. Manufacturing, B2B, food/beverage and FMCG pages explain distinct scopes without claiming completed campaigns. Concentrate active publishing on real estate and restaurants first; deepen the next sector only when an actual brief or relevant expertise supports it.

All pages remain on daas.media. Distribution means marketing reach and enquiry journeys, not stock logistics. Food/beverage content concerns product understanding; FMCG content concerns consumer and trade communication. Avoid additional near-duplicate local SEO or lead-generation pages that compete with the existing service pages.

## First 90 days

Days 1–14: deploy the reviewed branch, connect canonical domain and HTTPS, verify Search Console, inspect home/real-estate/restaurant/resource URLs, submit sitemap, test enquiry delivery and record a dated baseline. Check domain variants, redirects, canonical tags, robots, mobile rendering and live PageSpeed. Search Console requires owner access or DNS verification; no verification token has been invented.

Days 15–30: inspect the two demonstrations and edit them with the founder's real perspective. Use the restaurant checklist with one authorised real business. Offer one tightly scoped pilot using /pilot/. Agree fee, deliverables, revisions, timetable, owner approvals and acceptance before starting. Case-study permission is a separate request, not a condition of service.

Days 31–60: deliver that work, record actual before/after evidence, publish two substantial supporting guides based on questions encountered. Repurpose each into useful posts on accounts the business owns. No bulk posting or automated outreach. Offer one relevant educational resource to a suitable community only after a specific recipient and message are approved.

Days 61–90: review non-brand queries, landing-page clicks, genuine enquiries and qualification. Improve promising pages before expanding. Publish a real case study only with permission and evidence; otherwise publish a transparent account of what was built and learned, without implying a measured business uplift.

## Weekly operating routine

- Review Search Console page/query data and index coverage; note data lag.
- Record genuine enquiries, source confidence, qualification, discussion and outcome separately.
- Fix one important customer-information or conversion gap.
- Create or improve one substantive asset when there is something useful to add; there is no mandatory publishing quota.
- Identify a small number of relevant editorial/community opportunities and assess fit before outreach.
- Log changes with date, hypothesis, evidence and next review point.

## Measuring honestly

Use the downloadable enquiry tracker. Source categories: Organic search, Organic social, Referral, Direct, Offline, Unknown. Record self-reported attribution as self-reported. An email-link click or downloaded brief is not an enquiry; an enquiry is not qualified by default. Exclude tests and spam. Compare like periods and note changes in offer, availability or seasonality.

No analytics trackers are installed. The contact form adds an optional “How did you hear about us?” field. Search Console measures search visibility after verification; it cannot identify each lead. A future analytics implementation needs an agreed provider and accurate privacy notice. Direct inbox delivery needs the existing Resend integration configured and tested.

Target good mobile experience: LCP <=2.5s, INP <=200ms, CLS <=0.1, evaluated with real-user data when available. A new low-traffic site may have no field dataset. Lab scores are diagnostic and are not evidence of rankings or conversions.

## Credibility without clients

Use a real founder name and biography when supplied. Show original methods and inspectable sample work. Demonstrations remain prominently labelled fictional; do not rebrand them as case studies. Publish real metrics only with timeframe, baseline, method, limitations and permission. Do not add fake reviews, awards, partner badges, author identities or office addresses.

## Off-page work

Create resources worth referring to. Seek relevant editorial mentions, accurate profiles and genuine collaborations; no paid-link packages, link exchanges or exact-match anchor demands. Community participation is not a guaranteed backlink. Existing client attribution is only relevant once a genuine engagement exists.
