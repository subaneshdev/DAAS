# Restaurant discovery checklist
DaaS.media | Practical review, not a ranking score
Business: [actual business] | Reviewer: [name] | Date: [date]

## Accurate information
- [ ] Name agrees across the website and authorised profiles.
- [ ] Opening and holiday hours are current.
- [ ] Address, directions and contact details have been verified.
- [ ] Dine-in / takeaway / delivery information is accurate.
- [ ] Owner has verified accessibility and facilities information.

## Menu and content
- [ ] Menu is readable on a phone and current.
- [ ] Prices are supplied and approved by the business.
- [ ] Dietary/allergen information is verified, not inferred.
- [ ] Photos represent current products and premises.
- [ ] Usage permissions are confirmed for imagery and identifiable people.
- [ ] Expired offers have been removed.

## Customer journey
- [ ] Menu, ordering and enquiry links work.
- [ ] Group booking instructions explain what information is needed.
- [ ] A real person owns enquiries and knows the response process.
- [ ] Test enquiries are labelled and excluded from reporting.
- [ ] Review invitations go to actual customers without incentives.
- [ ] No fictional listing or unapproved profile has been created.
